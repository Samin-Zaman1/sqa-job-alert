Checking session…
Uses your account through a separate Chrome profile in this folder, slowly, a few searches every few hours. LinkedIn doesn't allow automated access to logged-in pages and can restrict accounts that use it; if it shows a security check, this stops and asks you to log in again.
Post searches
Each one is a search of the past week's posts. Posts are kept only if they mention a place in Bangladesh, are hiring (not #OpenToWork), and name a QA role your filters accept.
